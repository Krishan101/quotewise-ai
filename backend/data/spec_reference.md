# QuoteWise AI - Materials Specification Reference

DEMO REFERENCE: summary compiled for the QuoteWise AI hackathon demo. Verify against the official ASTM / ASME documents before commercial use.

Each line below is one self-contained fact so a single search result carries the full claim.

## Stainless steel - flat products (ASTM A240)

- Grade 304 is a Stainless Steel grade. ASTM A240 covers Stainless Steel grade 304 sheet, plate and strip.
- Grade 304L is a Stainless Steel grade. ASTM A240 covers Stainless Steel grade 304L sheet, plate and strip.
- Grade 316 is a Stainless Steel grade. ASTM A240 covers Stainless Steel grade 316 sheet, plate and strip.
- Grade 316L is a Stainless Steel grade. ASTM A240 covers Stainless Steel grade 316L sheet, plate and strip.
- Grade 321 is a Stainless Steel grade. ASTM A240 covers Stainless Steel grade 321 sheet, plate and strip.
- Grade 347 is a Stainless Steel grade. ASTM A240 covers Stainless Steel grade 347 sheet, plate and strip.
- Grade 410 is a Stainless Steel grade. ASTM A240 covers Stainless Steel grade 410 sheet, plate and strip.
- Grade 430 is a Stainless Steel grade. ASTM A240 covers Stainless Steel grade 430 sheet, plate and strip.
- Grade 2205 is a Stainless Steel (duplex) grade. ASTM A240 covers Stainless Steel grade 2205 sheet, plate and strip.
- ASME SA240 is the ASME adoption of ASTM A240 for Stainless Steel sheet, plate and strip.

## Stainless steel - long products

- ASTM A276 covers Stainless Steel bar (grades 304, 304L, 316, 316L, 410). ASTM A276 does not cover sheet or plate.
- ASTM A479 covers Stainless Steel bar for boilers and pressure vessels. ASTM A479 does not cover sheet or plate.
- ASTM A312 covers Stainless Steel seamless and welded pipe (grades 304, 304L, 316, 316L). ASTM A312 does not cover sheet or plate.
- ASTM A554 covers Stainless Steel welded mechanical tube. ASTM A554 does not cover sheet or plate.

## Carbon steel

- Grade A36 is a Carbon Steel structural grade. ASTM A36 covers Carbon Steel grade A36 plate, bar and shapes.
- Grade A572 is a Carbon Steel high-strength low-alloy grade. ASTM A572 covers Carbon Steel grade A572 plate and shapes.
- Grade 1018 is a Carbon Steel grade, normally supplied as cold finished bar.
- Grade 1045 is a Carbon Steel grade, normally supplied as bar.
- Grade 4140 is a Carbon Steel (alloy steel) grade, normally supplied as bar.

## Aluminum

- Grade 6061 is an Aluminum grade. ASTM B209 covers Aluminum grade 6061 sheet and plate (tempers T6, T651, O).
- Grade 5052 is an Aluminum grade. ASTM B209 covers Aluminum grade 5052 sheet and plate (tempers H32, H34, O).
- Grade 3003 is an Aluminum grade. ASTM B209 covers Aluminum grade 3003 sheet and plate (tempers H14, O).
- Grade 7075 is an Aluminum grade. ASTM B209 covers Aluminum grade 7075 sheet and plate (tempers T6, T651).
- ASTM B221 covers Aluminum extruded bar, rod, profiles and tube. ASTM B221 does not cover sheet or plate.

## Product forms

- Form Sheet: flat product, thickness from 0.005 in up to under 0.1875 in (3/16 in).
- Form Plate: flat product, thickness 0.1875 in (3/16 in) and above.
- Form Coil: sheet or strip wound into a coil.
- Form Bar: solid round, square, hex or flat long product.
- Form Tube: hollow product specified by outside diameter and wall.
- Form Pipe: hollow product specified by nominal pipe size and schedule.

## Stainless and carbon sheet gauge (Manufacturers' Standard Gauge, nominal)

- Gauge 10ga sheet is 0.135 in thick.
- Gauge 11ga sheet is 0.120 in thick.
- Gauge 12ga sheet is 0.105 in thick.
- Gauge 14ga sheet is 0.075 in thick.
- Gauge 16ga sheet is 0.060 in thick.
- Gauge 18ga sheet is 0.048 in thick.
- Gauge 20ga sheet is 0.036 in thick.

## Stainless finishes

- Finish 2B is a cold rolled, annealed, pickled and skin-passed Stainless Steel sheet finish.
- Finish BA is a bright annealed Stainless Steel sheet finish.
- Finish No.4 is a brushed (polished) Stainless Steel sheet finish.
- Finish No.1 is a hot rolled, annealed and pickled (HRAP) Stainless Steel plate finish.
- Finish Mill is the as-produced finish for Aluminum sheet and plate.
